CMC Power & Lighting Guides

Practical guides on home backup power, commercial LED lighting and industrial generators for Pakistan, from the three brands of CMC Group.

Home backup power: Hyundai Power

Load-shedding still decides how Pakistani households plan their evenings, and the right backup starts with the running load: the fridge, fans, lights and a phone charger, not the headline wattage on the box. Sized that way, most homes find their answer among petrol generators for home rather than a larger unit that burns fuel at half load.

The wider Hyundai Power range adds inverter ACs, LED lighting and service and warranty support for households across Pakistan.

Commercial and industrial lighting: Hyundai LED Lights

Warehouses, factory yards, petrol stations and building facades need fittings that hold up to dust, summer heat and monsoon rain. For perimeter, signage and yard lighting on those sites, LED flood lights are the usual starting point.

For offices, retail and project specification, Hyundai LED Lights also supplies panel, down, tube and high-bay fittings with B2B quotations.

Industrial generators: Enpower

When a factory, hospital or commercial building needs standby power, the decision rests on load profile, duty cycle and fuel planning. Those are the questions to settle before comparing diesel generators for business and industrial sites.

Enpower supplies Perkins, Baudouin and Cummins powered generator sets, with sizing advice and quotations prepared per project.
