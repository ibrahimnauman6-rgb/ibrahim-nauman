Power, cooling and lighting, explained for Pakistan.

Practical guides on home backup power, commercial LED lighting and industrial generators from the three brands of CMC Group.

Our brands

Home backup

Hyundai Power

Load-shedding still decides how Pakistani households plan their evenings. Size backup to the running load, the fridge, fans and lights, and most homes land on petrol generators for home rather than an oversized unit.

The wider Hyundai Power range adds inverter ACs, LED lighting and service and warranty support.

Commercial lighting

Hyundai LED Lights

Warehouses, factory yards and petrol stations need fittings that survive dust, heat and monsoon rain, which is why LED flood lights are the usual starting point.

For offices and project specification, Hyundai LED Lights supplies panel, down, tube and high-bay fittings with B2B quotations.

Industrial power

Enpower

Standby power for a factory or hospital rests on load profile, duty cycle and fuel planning, the questions to settle before comparing diesel generators for business.

Enpower supplies Perkins, Baudouin and Cummins powered sets, with sizing advice and quotations per project.
